About
Scientist. Entrepreneur.
Builder.

I'm a biomedical researcher and entrepreneur — trained in the molecular biology of aging across leading European research institutions, now building ventures that move the needle on human healthspan. For me, science and entrepreneurship aren't two different paths. They're the same mission.

My path took me from studying protein phosphorylation and yeast longevity in Scotland to neurodegeneration research in Portugal, autophagy and aging in the UK, and collective behaviour at the Max Planck Institute in Germany.

Along the way I built AI agents, launched an AI wellness bot called Mira, and developed deep fluency in the tools — from n8n automation to Python and bioinformatics — that let a small team move fast.

Today I'm building a longevity-focused space that combines the diagnostics, therapies, and AI infrastructure to help people genuinely extend their healthspan — not as a side project, but as the main event.
